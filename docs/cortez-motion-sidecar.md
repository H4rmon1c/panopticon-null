# Cortez preliminary final-reading vote

The separate `pnull-cortez-motion-v1` snapshot records the March 23, 2021 Cortez City Council final-reading motion on Ordinance 1286. Its seven yes votes are the item-specific table on physical PDF page 2; the council roster and meeting roles are on page 1. The page 1 consent vote is separate. The source describes proposed purchases of police equipment from Axon; the minutes do not establish execution, delivery, deployment, or office terms. The card can include Orly Lucero's separately sourced portrait from a 2017 city report and three newspaper image derivatives described in [the portrait provenance note](cortez-press-portraits.md). Their photo dates are unknown; the other three portraits remain unavailable.

`scripts/cortez-motion-sidecar.py` binds the preserved original PDF and extracted text to fixed hashes and exact text, the city index and its archived anchor, both capture receipts, and a current non-historical research record. Self-host publication regenerates the sidecar from the private research store and requires its source record in the newly staged research snapshot. Missing or changed evidence yields a `not_available` marker; no private archive or raw receipt details are published; receipt digests are public. The checked-in sanitized snapshot lets the static terminal display the available case, while the browser independently verifies the same research binding before rendering. An unavailable record does not assert that the source disproved the vote.

The sidecar is separate from `pnull-research-v1` and does not change that schema. An exact-source change requires re-examination of both the original visual table and the source chain before updating fixed digests or claims. This process does not establish statewide completeness.
