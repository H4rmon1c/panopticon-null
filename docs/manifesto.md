# Manifesto

> We think, therefore we are free. No corporation or state acquires moral title to the inner life, movements, associations, or ordinary existence of a human person merely because technology makes collection cheap.

Panopticon Null opposes systems that convert ordinary life into permanent dossiers. Surveillance thrives through secrecy, complexity, institutional inertia, and the assumption that resistance is futile. We make it visible, legible, contestable, and politically expensive.

We do this through lawful public records, reproducible analysis, source-linked evidence, privacy-preserving publication, and free software available to everyone.

Our mission is to lawfully dismantle the global surveillance panopticon by making every acquisition visible, every promise permanent, every abuse provable, and every community capable of saying no.

The machinery is institutional, not inevitable. Procurement records expose what authorities intend to acquire. Agendas expose when decisions can be contested. Contracts expose promises and costs. Amendments expose expansion. Policies expose claimed boundaries. Preserved versions expose what changed or disappeared.

Confrontation without evidence is noise. Evidence without purpose is administration. Panopticon Null exists to join a clear moral purpose to records that can withstand hostile review.

## v0.0.2: the verifiable receipt

Version 0.0.2 sharpens the standard of proof. Every citation is page-accurate and bound to exact geometry and digests. Every processing run leaves an immutable provenance record. Every public citation and image excerpt passes a human review gate. Subjects and actions are kept distinct, so an ordinance approval is never mistaken for a vendor purchase. Uncertain X threads are reconciled by an operator, never blindly retried. The demo produces only dry-run drafts and zero network posts.

We do not assert what the public record does not prove, and we do not automate access to restricted portals. Where the reviewed source cannot prove a procurement, we say so plainly rather than fabricate a relationship.

No human being is born to be indexed.
