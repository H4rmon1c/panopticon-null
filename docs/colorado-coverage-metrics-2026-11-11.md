# Colorado coverage measures — November 11, 2026

At 21:47 UTC, the private collector snapshot recorded at least one completed
document or portal screen for **545 of 4,111 mapped entries (13.3%)**. This is
an activity measure, not the percentage of Colorado decisions or records
covered. Neither a complete statewide universe of relevant decisions nor a
complete lookback window has been established. The geography includes 3,595
provisional special-district entries and does not represent every public body.

For counties and municipalities combined, 79 of 337 (23.4%) had at least one
screen. Across the full roster, 1,580 entries (38.4%) had an attempted website
check; 967 (23.5%) had a reached website. The snapshot contained 283 preliminary
references. These are not counts of approvals or distinct decisions. Failure,
redirect, an empty search, and a reached homepage do not establish absence or
complete coverage.

A separate private shared-host reconnaissance attempted 327 hosts associated
with 1,398 provisional district rows, reached 150 sites and found 92 exact
names on pages. Sixty-eight directly linked documents were screened with no
keyword match. These host-level observations do not establish which district
owns a document and are excluded from the table. The private EFF fallback and
its 381 third-party discovery leads are also excluded. See
[tenant reconnaissance](colorado-district-tenant-reconnaissance.md) and
[third-party discovery](colorado-third-party-discovery.md).
