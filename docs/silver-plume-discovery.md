# Silver Plume: small-town document-index pilot

The [Town of Silver Plume homepage](https://silverplumetown.com/) describes a population of 203. Its [agendas and minutes index](https://silverplumetown.com/agendas-and-minutes/) publishes same-host PDF agendas, packets, and approved minutes, including older Board of Trustees records. The town maps to the atlas geography ID `us-co-place-0870360`. This profile adds **private source reach only**: no Silver Plume vote, contract, portrait, or surveillance decision has been established or released.

`configs/states/co-silver-plume.toml` configures the exact HTML index and a separate `/wp-content/uploads/` PDF path policy. `pnull source discover-index silver-plume-document-index` archives the HTML and writes digest-bound, private candidates. It accepts only same-host HTTPS upload PDFs, no query or fragment, with a 2,048-character URL limit and a 2,000-candidate limit. `source archive-candidate` then re-parses the preserved HTML, checks its fetch receipt and reviewed policy, and archives **one** chosen PDF with raw bytes, digest, and receipt. A changed response at the same URL is retained as a new version. The index and path policy are **unscheduled** until their separate source-level robots and terms reviews are recorded.

An exact two-element HTML excerpt from a manual 2026-11-10 research read is pinned in [`index-excerpt.json`](../fixtures/co-silver-plume/index-excerpt.json) and used in the parser regression. The full-page SHA-256 is recorded there for research traceability; the full page is not a reviewed private capture. The current index exposed 106 same-host upload-PDF links in that read. That count is a parser check, not an investigated case count.

The town's minutes may describe a motion as “all in favor” without naming each individual vote. Attendance plus that phrase does not establish a person-by-person roll call. Where an official API or a later document supplies a named vote, it can be archived as a narrow fallback and cited; this profile does not claim that such an API exists. Until then, individual vote attribution remains **not established**. The town's [Boards & Committees page](https://silverplumetown.com/boards-and-committees/) lists terms ending in April 2026 and cannot establish who held office in November 2026. The same evidence rule applies to portraits: the officeholder and image must be linked by a sourced identity record before display, and no such portrait is included in this pilot.

The next step is a bounded scheduler for reviewed indexes and PDF candidates, followed by local document extraction and exact-locator matter/vote detection. [Moffat and Simla](moffat-simla-discovery.md) now have separate same-host upload-path profiles; towns on other publishing platforms still need narrow adapters. The [Colorado worklist](colorado-worklist.md) marks Silver Plume as `document_index_configured` so it is visible without suggesting that records are already monitored or published.
