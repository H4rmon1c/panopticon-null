# Legal and ethical boundaries

Panopticon Null uses lawful public records and public endpoints. It does not bypass authentication, access controls, CAPTCHAs, paywalls, session restrictions, robot exclusions, or technical restrictions. It does not scrape private systems. A blocked or ambiguous portal is documented and left alone.

The software provides no capability or instructions to damage, disable, evade, interfere with, or physically locate surveillance equipment. It performs no facial recognition, plate tracking, person-level movement analysis, or identity dossier construction.

A finding reports source text and a deterministic classification. A diff reports a textual change. Neither is a legal opinion. The system may identify a possible rule or policy discrepancy for human review, but it does not declare conduct unlawful, unconstitutional, abusive, corrupt, or malicious.

Operators are responsible for reviewing current terms, robots directives, public-record restrictions, copyright, privacy obligations, and publication risk in their jurisdiction. Configuration is not legal advice.
