Subject:
title
FLOCK Camera Contract 2025
end

Purpose and Request:
recommended action
The Sheriff's Office requests the Board of County Commissioners approve and authorize the Chair of the County Commissioners to sign the Agreement (contract extension) between Arapahoe County and FLOCK for continued access to the license plate reader camera system.
end

Background and Discussion: Throughout 2024, the Arapahoe County Sheriff's Office has been under a trial period of 25 mounted FLOCK license plate reader cameras. The camera system was permitted through Arapahoe County/City of Centennial and, over the past year, has proven very valuable in locating stolen vehicles and wanted subjects in our service area. When we entered into this contract with FLOCK over a year ago, it was laid out in detail that we would use the cameras for the one-year trial period after which we would pay for the cameras each year at the cost of $3,000.00 per camera.

With the current budget constraints we are facing, we asked for, and received, authorization from the Arapahoe County Sheriff's Office Forfeiture Committee to use seizure funds to pay for the cameras in 2025. With that, after several discussions with FLOCK, we were given a quote for $65,500.00 for camera service in 2025. This is a $10,000 discount from our original quote. The recommendation and request from the agency is that we sign the contract for 2025 and fund the cameras using approved seizure funds.

Alternatives: The alternative is for the Sheriff's Office to discontinue use of the FLOCK cameras which would not only negate all the installation and permitting work that has been done but would negatively impact operations as we would no longer receive notifications of stolen vehicles and wanted subjects in the areas we provide police services to.
