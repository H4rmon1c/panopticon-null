# Commerce City primary-source evidence

These are the exact response bytes fetched on 2026-11-10 from the city's Legistar records. `SHA256SUMS` records each preserved file's digest. The files document two distinct decisions, not proof that either contract was later executed.

## December 4, 2023: initial Flock award authorization

- [Council event](https://webapi.legistar.com/v1/commerce/Events/3644?EventItems=1&AgendaNote=1&MinutesNote=1&EventItemAttachments=1): `event-3644-2023-12-04.json`, `$.EventDate`, `$.EventBodyName`, and `$.EventItems[17]`. The item is `Res 2023-135` (EventItemId `56805`); its title authorizes award of a contract to Flock Group, Inc., its action is `adopted`, and its status is `Passed`. `$.EventItems[17].EventItemConsent` is `1`.
- [Consent Agenda votes](https://webapi.legistar.com/v1/commerce/EventItems/56779/Votes): `votes-56779-consent-2023-12-04.json`, `$[0..8].VotePersonName`, `.VoteValueName`, and `.VoteEventItemId`. Seven voted Aye; two were Excused. These are votes on the **consent agenda** (`$.EventItems[10]` of the event), which included the Flock resolution. They are not a standalone roll call on that resolution. The [resolution item's own votes endpoint](https://webapi.legistar.com/v1/commerce/EventItems/56805/Votes) returned `[]`.
- [Resolution No. 2023-135](https://legistar.granicus.com/Commerce/attachments/f96e68eb-2de2-491a-a635-6f64f2f8779d.pdf): `res-2023-135-resolution.pdf`, page 1 recitals and §§2–4; page 2 passage line. It names Flock and automatic license plate readers, gunshot detection, and cameras. Section 2 approves an award **subject to successful contract finalization**; §4 authorizes later execution. The attached draft contract is not evidence of execution.
